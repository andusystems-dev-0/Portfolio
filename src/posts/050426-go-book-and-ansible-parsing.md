## New Go book!
I recently picked up Learning Go -- An idiomatic Approach to Real-World Go Programming by Jon Bodner. Excited to start reading this and referencing it in my future CodeGrotto project. I chose Go due to the ease of writing, industry adoption, and speed. Go comes in as a close second to Rust (my second option) in speed but is significantly more widely adopted by the industry. On top of this Go is what most of Kubernetes uses so I will use it heavily going foward for my personal projects that interact with my Kubernetes homelab clusters. Very excited to get to reading this.
